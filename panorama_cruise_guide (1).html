Port-by-port guide

These are independent-exploration ideas, not reserved tours. Restaurant hours and operating conditions should be checked shortly before departure.

Mar 2 · 9 AM–9 PM

Puerto Vallarta

Best for food + walking

Take an authorized taxi into Centro. Walk the Malecón, see the Church of Our Lady of Guadalupe, cross the Río Cuale, and explore Zona Romántica.

Food to seek out
  • Ceviche or aguachile at a local seafood spot
  • Traditional pozole
  • Neighborhood taco stands on a guided food walk

Spend: Small-group food tour. Skip: All-inclusive beach pass.

Mar 3 · 9:30 AM–6 PM

Mazatlán

Best for history

Head to Centro Histórico using authorized port transportation. Explore the cathedral, Mercado Pino Suárez, Plaza Machado, and Ángela Peralta Theater, then stroll toward Olas Altas.

Food to seek out
  • Sinaloan shrimp dishes
  • Seafood soup and tostadas
  • Local seafood near the historic center

Spend: Historical walking guide. Skip: Golden Zone shopping excursion.

Mar 4 · 9 AM–6 PM

La Paz

Best for a slower day

The ship docks at Pichilingue, outside town. Allow time for transport to central La Paz. Visit the cathedral and Jardín Velasco, check the Regional Museum of Anthropology and History, and walk the Malecón.

Food to seek out
  • Baja-style fish tacos
  • Fresh oysters or grilled seafood
  • A relaxed waterfront lunch

Spend: Local culinary guide if desired. Skip: Trying to fit Balandra, a boat trip, and downtown into one day.

Mar 5 · 6:30 AM–1:30 PM

Cabo San Lucas

Best for whales

Book an early whale-watching trip with a reputable operator. This is a tender port with a short visit, so leave plenty of time to return. Alternatively, take a short boat trip to El Arco and eat fish tacos near the marina.

What to look for
  • Humpback whales during migration season
  • El Arco and Land's End
  • Early return to the tender pier

Spend: Early small-boat whale tour. Skip: Long drive to San José del Cabo on this short stop.

Onboard tips

Oceanview cabin

Bring magnetic hooks, a USB night-light, and packing cubes. Use public decks for the scenery, especially near Cabo.

First afternoon

Keep medications, documents, swimwear and essentials in your carry-on. Checked luggage may arrive later.

Food + entertainment

Check the HUB app for SkyRide, comedy, Serenity adults-only deck, and specialty restaurants. Confirm current opening times and charges onboard.

Cell service

Use airplane mode at sea and enable Wi-Fi separately if needed. Maritime cellular roaming can be expensive.

Weather + seas

Expect breezy, cooler Pacific sea days. If prone to motion sickness, prepare before symptoms start.

Documents + boarding

Depart from Long Beach Cruise Terminal by the Queen Mary, not San Pedro. Check Carnival's current identification and prohibited-item policies.
